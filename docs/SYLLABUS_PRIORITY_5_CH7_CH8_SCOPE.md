# Syllabus Priority 5 — Chapters 7 & 8 Scope Review (Corrected)

Completed 2026-08-19. Determines whether Chapters 7 (Vehicle Registration and Insurance) and 8 (Motorcycles) belong in the Class 7 motor-vehicle learner syllabus.

**This is a corrected report.** The initial version incorrectly claimed that "only Chapters 1–6 are in Class 7 scope" based on an unsupported inference. This revision is based on actual reading of Chapter 7 and proper separation of official scope from project curriculum decisions.

## Official Class 7 scope

The official Nova Scotia Class 7 knowledge-test page states:

> "You need to review the Driver's Handbook to prepare for the Knowledge Test. There are 2 parts to the test: Rules of the Road Test, Road Sign Recognition Test."

> "The Rules of the Road Test assesses your knowledge of the traffic laws and rules for safe driving."

The test page also notes:

> "Right now, you can only take the Knowledge Test online for a Motorcycle Learner Licence, Learner's Licence (class 7) or Farm Tractor Licence (class 8)."

**What the official source establishes:**
- The Driver's Handbook is the study material
- The test covers "traffic laws and rules for safe driving" and "road signs"
- Motorcycle licensing is a separate test pathway

**What the official source does NOT establish:**
- No explicit chapter-number exclusion (e.g., "Chapters 1–6 only")
- No statement that Chapters 7–8 are excluded from the test

## Project scope interpretation

The project must make concept-level curriculum decisions about which Handbook material is appropriate for the Class 7 app. These decisions are based on:
- Whether the concept is a durable driver responsibility
- Whether it is testable as single-best-answer
- Whether it is owner administration vs. driver operation
- Whether it is volatile administrative detail

**This is a curriculum judgment, not an official Province rule.**

## Conclusion

### Chapter 7

**PARTIALLY RELEVANT — DRIVER DUTIES INCLUDED, OWNER/ADMINISTRATION EXCLUDED**

Chapter 7 contains genuine driver duties (carry permit, carry insurance, carry inspection report) that are confirmed by current MVA and warrant Class 7 assessment. One question was added to cover these durable driver responsibilities.

Owner administration (registration process, fees, renewal timelines, transfer paperwork) is excluded as not relevant to driver operation knowledge.

Volatile administrative details (fine amounts, renewal periods by regulation) are excluded to avoid testing unstable numbers.

### Chapter 8

**MOTORCYCLE-LICENCE MATERIAL — EXCLUDED EXCEPT SHARED-ROAD DUTIES**

Chapter 8 was actually read (pp. 177–204). It contains:
- Motorcycle licensing levels (Class LM, 6N, 6) and restrictions
- Motorcycle operation techniques (braking, countersteering, cornering)
- Motorcycle safety equipment (helmets, protective clothing)
- Motorcycle pre-ride checks
- Motorcycle special situations (skids, wobble, blowouts, stuck throttle)
- Motorcycle passenger carrying and group riding

All of this is motorcycle-rider-specific material belonging to the separate motorcycle learner licence pathway.

The chapter contains only two motorist-facing concepts (both on page 185):
1. "Drivers fail to see motorcyclists" — already covered by general blind-spot questions
2. "Motorcycles entitled to full lane" — already covered by rules-share-001

No new questions were added for Chapter 8.
