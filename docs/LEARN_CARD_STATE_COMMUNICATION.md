# Learn card state communication

Learn cards show two separate ideas:

- coverage: how many assessed questions or sign concepts have been seen;
- mastery state: the learner's cumulative evidence for Not started, Learning,
  Developing, Complete, or Mastered.

The coverage bar remains a truthful coverage indicator. A full bar means all
available material has been seen; it does not by itself mean the topic is
Complete.

Intermediate states are written on the card as badges. Developing also shows
the cumulative accuracy and the Complete threshold, for example:

> 67% accuracy · 80% needed to complete

Accuracy is cumulative learner evidence. A perfect latest retry can therefore
be celebrated on the session result screen while the Learn card remains
Developing until the historical accuracy reaches the Complete threshold. A
retry adds new evidence; it does not erase earlier incorrect answers.

This communication is shared by Rules topics and canonical Road Sign
categories. Complete and Mastered remain distinct: Complete is the ordinary
progression threshold, while Mastered requires stronger repeated and retained
evidence.
